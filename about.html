Our Story

From Seed to Cup

Founded in 2024, Onyx & Oak began with a simple mission: to elevate the daily coffee ritual. Frustrated by mass-produced, stale coffee, our founders traveled to the highlands of Colombia and Ethiopia to build direct relationships with farmers.

Today, we roast our beans in small batches right here in the city, ensuring that every bag and every cup delivers the vibrant, complex flavors nature intended.
